# SnippIt icon

The new icon is a folded white paper ribbon forming an S on an ink-blue tile.
The larger silhouette stays legible at Dock and sidebar sizes.

- Method: built-in imagegen tool, with a transparent background.
- Original generated asset: [SnippIt-icon.png](SnippIt-icon.png).
- App asset catalog: `SnippIt/Assets.xcassets/SnippItIcon.appiconset`.
- Sizes 16–1024 px were derived with macOS `sips`; alpha was preserved.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished macOS app icon for a simple private plain-text notes app called SnippIt. Create one exceptionally clean, sophisticated icon, square canvas 1024 by 1024. Center a single bold white paper ribbon, folded into a subtle abstract S shape, on a deep ink-blue rounded-square tile. The symbol should read as both a small slip of paper and an S, using only 2 broad connected folded surfaces, a crisp silhouette, generous negative space, almost flat with a very soft restrained paper fold shadow. Quiet premium native Mac aesthetic. The tile occupies roughly 82 percent of the canvas width and height, optically centered with consistent transparent margins on every side, macOS-style continuous rounded corners. Saturated ink blue, warm white, just a subtle lighter blue on the folded edge. Strong clarity at 16 and 32 pixels. NO text, NO typography, NO pencil, NO small text lines, NO border, NO badges, NO extra shapes, NO scene or mockup, NO exaggerated gloss, NO rainbow or purple gradients. Deliver the standalone finished icon on a genuinely transparent canvas outside the tile.
